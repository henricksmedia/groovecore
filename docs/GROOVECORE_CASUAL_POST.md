# 🎵 Just dropped something fun...

Hey everyone! 👋

Been jamming late nights and built this little TR-808 drum machine in the browser. No downloads, no BS - just pure beat-making vibes.

---

## 🔥 **What I cooked up:**

🥁 **Real 808/909 sounds** (not samples - actual synthesis!)  
🎹 **Click to make beats** - 16 steps of pure groove  
🎵 **100+ patterns from hit songs** - learn from the legends  
💾 **Export to your DAW** - take it further if you want  
🎨 **Genre presets** - trap, hip-hop, house, whatever you're feeling

## 💭 **Why I made this:**

Honestly? I love the 808 sound but don't always have my hardware setup. Wanted something I could just open and start vibing with. Plus, thought it'd be cool to study how different genres use rhythm patterns.

Turned into this whole thing with educational content and professional features... but at its heart, it's just about making beats and having fun! 🎶
