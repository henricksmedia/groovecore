GrooveCore Help

Getting started

  • Program a beat: click step buttons to toggle hits on and off (velocity 100). Shift+click writes an accent hit (127), Alt+click a ghost (45).
  • Shape a hit: drag vertically on an active step to set its velocity (1-127). Right-click (or long-press on touch) a step for ratchet x2/x3/x4, flam, probability, and velocity tiers.
  • Accent row (AC): the last grid row flags whole steps as accented - every instrument on that step hits harder and pushes the master bus. The Accent knob sets how much.
  • Play: press PLAY or the Space bar. Tempo is the knob next to the BPM display (40-260 BPM), or tap the T key in time.
  • Explore sounds: click an instrument name to open its controls (level, gain, pan, tune, decay, tone, snappy, sends). Every knob is drag, wheel, and touch friendly; double-click resets it.
  • Find a groove: use BROWSE (or press P) to search 171 presets and grooves by genre and BPM, mark favorites, or hit Surprise me. Every load is undoable.

Your work is safe

  • Autosave: the session is stored locally as you edit and restored when you come back - closing the tab loses nothing.
  • Undo/redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y cover step edits, knob gestures, preset loads, clears, and chain edits (100 levels).
  • Projects: SAVE downloads a .groove.json file; LOAD brings it back (old io808 save files still load).
  • Share: the SHARE button copies a link that recreates your beat on any device - no account, no server.

Export

  • MIDI: the MIDI button opens the export menu - single track, multi-track (per-instrument, with level/pan CCs), or the full chain. 480 PPQ, exact velocities, deterministic: re-exports are byte-identical.
  • Audio: render the mix as WAV, or a zip of per-instrument stems that line up sample-accurately in your DAW.
  • What you hear is what you get: playback, MIDI, and audio renders all come from the same event list - swing, ratchets, flams, velocities and accents match exactly.

Play it live

  • Pads: the letter keys are drum pads (see the Shortcuts tab, or press ? any time).
  • MIDI in: MIDI IN connects a controller - note-on velocity is respected.
  • Record: REC arms while playing; pad and MIDI hits are quantized to the nearest 16th and written into the pattern.
  • Chain: the chain bar under the sequencer strings up to 8 pattern slots (A/B x 16) into a song, switched on the bar. DUP→ duplicates the current pattern into the next free slot.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? at any time for this list in an overlay. Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a text field or a dialog is open.

Global

  • Space - Play / Stop
  • T - Tap tempo
  • M - Metronome (Ctrl+M while pads are active; plain M is the MT pad)
  • Ctrl+Z - Undo  ·  Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y - Redo
  • Ctrl+S - Save project  ·  Ctrl+E - Export menu
  • ↑ / ↓ - BPM +1 / -1 (Shift: ±10)
  • 1 / 2 - Variation A / B
  • P - Preset browser
  • ? - Shortcut overlay  ·  Esc - Close dialogs

Finger drumming pads

  • Z X C V B N M , - BD  SD  CP  CH  OH  LT  MT  HT
  • A S D F G H J K L - RIM  CB  CL  MA  HC  MC  LC  CYM  CR
  • Shift + pad - Accent hit (velocity 127)
  • Alt + pad - Ghost hit (velocity 60)

Knob gestures

  • Drag - Turn knob (works with mouse, touch, and pen)
  • Shift + drag - Fine adjust (x0.1)  ·  Ctrl + drag - Coarse adjust (x3)
  • Double-click / double-tap - Reset knob to default
  • Mouse wheel - Nudge value ±0.3

Grid

  • Click step - Toggle hit on/off (100)  ·  Shift+click accent (127), Alt+click ghost (45)
  • Drag on an active step - Set velocity (1px = 1 velocity); drag sideways to paint
  • Right-click / long-press step - Ratchet, flam, probability, tier, clear
  • Tab + arrow keys - Navigate the grid; Enter toggles the focused step
  • Number row / instrument name - Click to bulk-toggle a column or row
  • CLEAR - Clear the pattern (drag across the number row to set pattern length)
